Featured diagnostic

Gate Diagnostic

Some projects are economically compelling but cannot yet cross the threshold into deployable institutional capital. A Gate Diagnostic identifies the actual constraint.

It establishes whether a specific capital structure can close, what is preventing it from closing, what the missing capital or evidence must accomplish, and the sequence required to move forward.

It asks:

  • Is the asset sufficiently real?
  • Is ownership sufficiently clear?
  • Is credible capital already interested?
  • What exact layer of risk can no existing party hold?
  • What would make the opportunity bankable or investable?
  • Who has to say yes next?
  • What evidence would cause them to do so?
