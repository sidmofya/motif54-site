1. Who we are

MOTIF 54 is operated by CXB Ventures LLC dba MOTIF 54, a California limited liability company (“MOTIF 54,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and handle information when you visit motif54.com, submit an enquiry, participate in one of our programs, or otherwise interact with us.

2. Information we collect

We may collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • your name
  • email address
  • organization
  • professional role
  • information you submit through website forms
  • information about a project, company, transaction, asset, or program enquiry
  • correspondence and other communications you send to us

We may also collect limited technical information automatically when you use the website, such as:

  • IP address
  • browser and device information
  • pages visited
  • referring pages or websites
  • approximate location derived from IP address
  • website usage and interaction data

The precise technical information collected will depend on the services and analytics tools actually implemented on the website.

3. How we use information

We may use information we collect to:

  • respond to enquiries
  • evaluate whether there is a fit for a project, program, or engagement
  • provide and administer our services and programs
  • communicate with you
  • operate, maintain, secure, and improve the website
  • understand how visitors use the website
  • prevent fraud, misuse, or security threats
  • comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • maintain appropriate business records
  • establish, exercise, or defend legal rights

We do not sell personal information.

4. Project and business information

If you submit information relating to a company, project, asset, transaction, fundraising process, investment opportunity, or other business matter, you should only provide information you are authorized to share.

Submission of information through the website does not by itself create a confidential, fiduciary, advisory, investment-management, broker-dealer, agency, or other professional relationship.

Do not submit highly confidential, proprietary, privileged, or commercially sensitive information through a general website form unless we have agreed on an appropriate method for receiving it.

5. Service providers

We may use third-party service providers to operate the website and our business.

These may include providers of:

  • website hosting
  • analytics
  • email
  • forms and communications
  • customer relationship management
  • scheduling
  • cloud infrastructure
  • security
  • other operational services

These providers may process information on our behalf in connection with the services they provide.

6. Analytics, cookies, and similar technologies

The website does not currently use cookies, analytics tools, advertising technologies, or similar tracking technologies. We do not set cookies on your device, and we do not operate a first-party or third-party analytics product on this website.

The website loads typefaces from Google Fonts, and enquiries submitted through the Work With Us form are transmitted to a Google service that records the submission and notifies us. Those requests involve your IP address and standard technical request data reaching Google as the provider of those services.

If analytics, advertising, or other non-essential tracking technologies are added, the website and this Privacy Policy will be updated to provide any notices, choices, or consent mechanisms required by applicable law.

7. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

This may include retaining information to:

Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the information and the context in which it was collected.

8. Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect information in our possession or control.

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. Accordingly, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Depending on where you live and the laws applicable to your information, you may have rights concerning your personal information.

These may include rights to:
