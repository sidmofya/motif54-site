About

Industrial questions. An African vantage point.

MOTIF 54 works where technology, physical infrastructure and capital meet. We help investors and industrial leaders understand the systems behind a decision, then make it.

What we work on

Four connected systems.

Intelligence

AI and the compute, power and cooling it requires.

Energy

The generation and grid capacity beneath industrial growth and computation.

Minerals

The resources and processing chains behind electrification and industry.

Capital

The financing, ownership and institutional conditions that decide what gets built.

Vantage point

Close to the ground.

Many industrial questions of this era run through Africa: its minerals, its power systems and its growing demand for computation. Working from that vantage point gives us context and access that a distant reading lacks. The questions we take on are industrial ones, wherever the decision sits.

Leadership

Sid Mofya

Sid Mofya is the founder of MOTIF 54. He previously served as Executive Director of the Draper Venture Network, a global alliance of venture capital firms, and as a Process Technologist with Royal Dutch Shell. He is a Kauffman Fellow and a chemical engineer by training.

His work has crossed venture capital, energy, critical minerals, AI infrastructure, and capital formation, with direct participation in projects and investment ecosystems across African markets.
