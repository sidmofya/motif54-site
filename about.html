About

MOTIF 54 works where capital, technology, and strategic African assets meet.

MOTIF 54 is an applied institution focused on building productive capacity and African ownership across AI infrastructure, energy, and critical minerals.

How we work

Three connected modes.

Projects

Projects create direct participation.

Intelligence

Intelligence improves the quality of consequential decisions.

Programs

Programs build the people and institutional capability required to carry those decisions forward.

The three reinforce one another. Participation produces better intelligence. Better intelligence produces better decisions. Programs distribute the resulting capability.

Leadership

Sid Mofya

Sid Mofya is the founder of MOTIF 54. He previously served as Executive Director of the Draper Venture Network, a global alliance of venture capital firms, and as a Process Technologist with Royal Dutch Shell. He is a Kauffman Fellow and a chemical engineer by training.

His work has crossed venture capital, energy, critical minerals, AI infrastructure, and capital formation, with direct participation in projects and investment ecosystems across African markets.
