The read

Assets and capital fail to meet.

Africa does not lack assets, capital demand, or strategic relevance. What is missing is a legible account of what is true, standing between the two.

The people holding the asset describe it in one language. The people who could fund it require another, and cannot verify the difference from where they sit. Someone has to hold that position between them — close enough to the asset to know what is actually there, fluent enough in institutional capital to know what it must become before anyone can commit.
