What AI Is Made Of

What must exist before a gigawatt of AI computation can operate?

Research by Sid Mofya · Interpretation by MOTIF 54

  • AI infrastructure
  • Power and cooling

A system of dependencies

An AI data centre is a system of dependencies. Electricity has to reach the site, be transformed and distributed, and remain available to the computers. Heat has to leave. The equipment that performs those jobs has its own material and manufacturing requirements.

This interactive reference model makes those relationships inspectable. It represents a hypothetical plant with one gigawatt of commissioned IT electrical capacity. It is not an operating facility, construction design or procurement schedule.

In words: electricity moves from the grid through power equipment to the computers, and the heat they produce must be removed. Materials and manufacturing supply every machine in that chain. Capital and permissions decide whether it is built at all.

Three observations

Each observation describes the public research. The interpretation beneath it is MOTIF 54’s reading for decision-makers, not a validated investment recommendation.

  1. The computers are part of a larger power system.

    The model distinguishes IT load from facility demand. The electricity the computers use is a different measure from what the whole plant draws from the grid. Follow the electricity and heat in the model.

    MOTIF interpretationAn infrastructure thesis should identify which demand measure it uses and what supporting capacity it assumes.

  2. Materials depend on design choices and scope.

    The active model distinguishes material scenarios from its inherited physical ledger, and each estimate applies to a stated equipment scope. Read how to interpret the results.

    MOTIF interpretationA material-demand estimate needs explicit equipment boundaries and sensitivity checks.

  3. Unknowns remain part of the decision.

    Buildings, offsite infrastructure and unresolved equipment are not silently counted as zero. The model keeps them open. See coverage and exclusions.

    MOTIF interpretationDefine the evidence still needed before relying on a modeled total.

Limits of the evidence

The physical reference and material estimates have different research histories. Run 04 material figures are estimates for partial equipment scope. Low, base and high are design cases, not probabilities or confidence intervals. Read the source’s assumptions before applying a result to a real asset.
